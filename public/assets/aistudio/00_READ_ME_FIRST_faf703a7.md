# PSS Acceptance 360 — Master Execution Notebook and User Manual v1.0

**Date:** 19 October 2026  
**Status:** Personal, pre-appointment research preparation — NOT a PSS instruction, approved SOP set, audit opinion, bank engagement mandate, purchase authorisation, or production approval.

This package is a **companion** to the immutable `PSS_A360_Master_Programme_Library_v1` and its 18 existing programme packages. Do not confuse the user manuals with the original evidence; source documents and their exact workbook tabs remain authoritative for their own content.

## Start here (first 30 minutes)

1. Read Volume A, pages 1–2, for boundaries and exact task IDs (P01–P18 personal; J01–J30 after joining).
2. Open `E_Master_Execution_Document_and_Evidence_Tracker_v1.xlsx` → `Start Here` → `Dashboard` → `48-Step Notebook`.
3. Review `Document Navigator` for the correct one of the 18 folders. Use Volume B for the *purpose and procedure* of each programme document.
4. If editing an existing workbook, use Volume C and its actual 191-sheet navigation index. Make a dated working copy; do not overwrite canonical source files.
5. When a job needs to be performed, check Volume D for the matching SOP; verify authority first, then record evidence and possible contrary findings.
6. Check the 25 portfolio-level `Evidence Gates` and six critical audit findings. Gate status must not change merely because a preliminary report exists.

The 32 roll-up jobs are a different level of aggregation from the 48 detailed original jobs. **Do not add them to create 80 unique tasks.** The 191 workbook tabs are the sum of 179 original programme workbook tabs plus 12 tabs in the more recent Master Control Register. None of these counts means work was executed.

## Progress and safety boundaries

- All task execution statuses begin `Not started`; no bank interviews, internal PSS tests, legal sign-off, assurance or investment approval is recorded as complete.
- Before appointment: public desk research, documentary preparation, source refresh and synthetic planning only.
- After formal appointment: obtain written research and data permissions separately from permission to speak with banks, perform tests, engage suppliers, execute a pilot or release capital.
- All original source packages and SHA-256 manifests stay unchanged. New edits require versioned copies, documented change requests and downstream F01 impact review.
- Do not paste real bank transaction records, names, credentials, or other restricted data into the personal tracker. Follow the institution's secure repository, DPA/consent and retention process once authorised.
- PSS Operate + Connect is only the preferred research hypothesis. Alternatives PSS Pay-led, partner-led, full suite and no-build remain open.

## Assumptions and caveats

Documents were inspected and worksheet names extracted from the existing Master Library. Tab instruction notes are operational guidance inferred from tab titles and source context, not proof of prior execution. F02/F03/F04/F05/F06 methods require official technical, legal, financial and independent review before managerial decision. This package is not a substitute for institution-approved policies, local legal advice or source-document instructions.
